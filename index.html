Ph.D., Electrical Engineering & Computer Science

Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University · Daytona Beach, FL

Ph.D. focused on UAS integration into the National Airspace System, Advanced Air Mobility, airspace routing and operations, flight-recorder requirements, aviation safety, optimization, simulation, and system-level modeling. My dissertation addressed noise-optimized routing for Advanced Air Mobility, building on a multidisciplinary background spanning electrical engineering, signal processing, wireless communications, and data-driven aerospace systems research.
