03 / Dissertation

Noise-optimized routes for air taxi.

A coupled route-network, trajectory-refinement, and acoustic-evaluation framework for Advanced Air Mobility.

Validated doctoral research 2026

Noise-Optimized Routes for Air Taxi

Developed a coupled vertiport-siting, route-network, trajectory-refinement, and acoustic-evaluation framework. The model compares baseline, horizontal, altitude, and integrated routing candidates under efficiency, balanced, noise, and sensitive-receptor priorities.

Noise-priority solutions reduced class-balanced Lmax,95 by up to approximately 3.9 dB and SEL95/EPNL95 by approximately 6 dB while preserving origin-destination structure at a modest route-length cost.

Four Methods for Noise-Aware Route Optimization

The dissertation evaluates route geometry, altitude, competing operational objectives, and schedule effects as connected parts of one planning framework rather than isolated analyses.

Together, the methods expose where acoustic benefit comes from, what operational penalty it introduces, and how route-level improvements change under efficiency, noise, sensitive-receptor, and time-of-day priorities.

Review the dissertation record ↗
01

Horizontal optimization

Shifts conflict-prone corridors away from sensitive receptors while preserving origin-destination structure.

02

Vertical optimization

Raises interior flight profiles to increase source-receiver separation and quantifies the associated mission-energy penalty.

03

Multi-objective selection

Compares baseline, horizontal, altitude, and integrated candidates across four operational priority profiles.

04

Time-of-day analysis

Separates route-geometry effects on single events from schedule and night-weighting effects on cumulative DNL.
